# Business benefit and validation plan

The intended user is a Malaysian café owner or purchasing manager who repeatedly checks ingredients, contacts suppliers and records approvals. DecisionOS puts inventory evidence, quote arithmetic, human approval and an audit record in one journey. It supports SDG **8.2** (productivity through technological upgrading) and **8.3** (SME development). These are intended contributions, not demonstrated economic effects.

## What is measured now

The synthetic reference inputs calculate 1.5 days of milk coverage, RM570/RM660 quote totals and a RM90 premium. Automated tests verify these outputs, owner permission checks and duplicate-order prevention. No user-time, business-revenue, waste or stockout reduction has been measured.

## Proposed pilot, not completed research

Use the same synthetic shortage and quotations for both tasks: first a spreadsheet/manual comparison and approval record, then DecisionOS. Alternate task order across testers to reduce practice effects. Record start/end time, arithmetic mistakes, missing approval information, unassisted task completion and any facilitator help. Use several consenting team/SME testers and report sample size and individual results; do not generalise a small convenience sample.

| Metric | Baseline | Proposed target | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time to complete a correct procurement draft | **Planning assumption: 10 minutes**, replace with actual manual median | At least 30% reduction: ≤7 minutes if baseline is 10 | Stopwatch/screen recording, fixed start/end definitions |
| Quote arithmetic errors | Unknown until baseline task | Zero in reference tasks | Compare submitted values to deterministic answer key |
| Complete approval records | Unknown until baseline task | 100% of approved drafts contain actor, reason and timestamp | Audit inspection, numerator/denominator |
| Unassisted first-time completion | Unknown | ≥80% of pilot testers | Completion and help log, sample size |

Percentage targets are hypotheses for the proposal, not achieved results. If no pilot is completed before submission, explicitly label the baseline estimated and outcomes unvalidated. Do not infer lost sales, guaranteed availability or financial returns from agent narratives.

Reuse would replace the connector and industry assumptions while retaining the decision/approval/audit contract. A possible future subscription per SME requires willingness-to-pay validation; no pricing or market-size claim is made here.
